\cvsection{Summary}

\begin{cvparagraph}
I trained as a computational modeller: my PhD built multi-scale simulations of biological tissue, and for the 15 years since I've built and looked after research software that scientists rely on every day, including Jupyter, conda and conda-forge. I've led ambitious technical work from first design to production: a computer-vision ML platform built from scratch, Nebari's move from an internal tool to a community project, and the security hardening of conda-forge's build infrastructure. I design, write and review code, most recently on conda's environment resolution and package verification, and I do my best work on open-ended problems alongside the researchers who will use the results.
